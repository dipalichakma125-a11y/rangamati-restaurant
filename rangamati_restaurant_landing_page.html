Local food guide

What Should You Try?

Rangamati’s food culture brings together familiar Bangladeshi favourites and regional hill flavours.

  • Fresh fish curry or fish fry
  • Bamboo-cooked chicken, where available
  • Rice, dal and seasonal vegetables
  • Local bhorta and traditional side dishes
  • Tea, fresh juice and light snacks

Menu items depend on the restaurant and season. Ask about ingredients and prices before ordering.
